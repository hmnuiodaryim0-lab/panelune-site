Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Our approach

Panelune is designed to be local-first. No user account is required, and Panelune does not operate a developer-controlled cloud service for your library.

Library and reading data

Imported books, library information, and reading data are stored locally on your device. Panelune does not sell personal information, use advertising trackers, or collect information for user profiling. We do not collect your email address through the app.

Private Library

Private Library stores selected books separately on your device and uses the device’s authentication to control access. It does not create a separate online account or upload those books to a developer-controlled cloud library.

Wi-Fi Transfer

When you use Wi-Fi Transfer, Panelune operates over your local network. The app may request local-network access for this feature. Files transferred through Wi-Fi Transfer are used for local import into Panelune; they are not sent to a developer-controlled cloud service.

In-app purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may process purchase information under Apple’s own privacy policies. Panelune does not receive or store your payment card details.

Removing your data

You can remove locally stored books and data through the normal controls in Panelune or on your device.

Contact

For product help, visit the Panelune Support page.
