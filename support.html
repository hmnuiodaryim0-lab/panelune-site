Panelune Support

Getting Started

  1. Open Wi-Fi Transfer in Panelune.
  2. Open the displayed local address on a computer connected to the same network.
  3. Choose whether you are importing comics or novels.
  4. Select files or folders to transfer.
  5. Open your library and start reading.

Supported Reading Modes

  • Page-by-page
  • Continuous vertical scrolling

Troubleshooting

  • Make sure your iPad and computer are on the same local network.
  • Keep Panelune open while a transfer is in progress.
  • If your computer cannot open the Wi-Fi Transfer address, restart Wi-Fi Transfer and try the displayed address again.
  • An unsupported or damaged file may not import. Check the file and try again.
  • If the issue continues, restart the app and retry the transfer.
