Getting started

  1. Open Wi-Fi Transfer in Panelune.
  2. Open the displayed local address on a computer connected to the same network.
  3. Choose whether you are importing comics or novels.
  4. Select files or folders to transfer.
  5. Open your library and start reading.

Common questions

Importing books

Use Wi-Fi Transfer to import supported books and folders from a computer on the same local network. Panelune supports PDF, EPUB, and comic archive files.

Wi-Fi Transfer

Make sure your iPad and computer are on the same local network, and keep Panelune open while a transfer is in progress. If the displayed address does not open, restart Wi-Fi Transfer and try the new address.

Linked Folders

Use Linked Folders to keep supported files in a folder and make them available to your Panelune library. Follow the in-app instructions when linking or updating a folder.

Reading PDF, EPUB, and comic archive files

Panelune is designed for reading supported PDF, EPUB, and comic archive files. An unsupported or damaged file may not import; check the file and try again.

Vertical scrolling

Panelune supports page-by-page reading and continuous vertical scrolling. Choose the reading mode that works best for the book you are reading.

Private Library

Your library is kept on your device. Panelune does not require an account, and Panelune does not provide books or copyrighted content. Users import or link their own files.

Troubleshooting

  • Restart the app and retry the action.
  • Check that your iPad has enough available storage.
  • Check that the file is supported and is not damaged.
  • For purchase issues, use Restore Purchases before contacting support.
